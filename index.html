HOW IT WORKS

Separate contributions from investment growth.

A recurring SIP is added monthly while the invested balance compounds using the monthly rate implied by the annual return assumption. If a step-up is entered, the monthly contribution increases once each year.

In goal mode, Carrowmont can either grow an amount entered in today’s money using the inflation / price-growth assumption or use a future target amount directly, then solve for the starting monthly SIP required.

In Time to target mode, you enter the target corpus and your SIP assumptions; the calculator estimates the first modelled month in which the portfolio reaches that target.

METHODOLOGY

Transparent calculations.

Monthly return: The annual return assumption is converted to the equivalent monthly compound rate.

SIP projection: Existing investments grow monthly. Contributions are added at month-end and can increase once each year by the entered step-up rate.

Future goal amount: Either today’s goal amount × (1 + inflation / price growth)years, or a future target entered directly.

Monthly SIP required: The starting monthly contribution that would model to the selected future goal while applying the entered annual step-up. It is solved numerically.

Time to target: The first modelled month in which the projected portfolio equals or exceeds the target corpus, checked for up to 50 years.

Important: SIP is a contribution method, not a guaranteed-return product. This calculator does not model market volatility, sequence of returns, taxes, fees, product-specific costs or investment suitability.
